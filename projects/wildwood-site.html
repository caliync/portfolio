Wildwood Site

The website for Wildwood Farm, built so players get the feel of the game before they press Play. It opens on a 3D forest with a smoking campfire, fireflies and ground mist.

  • three.js
  • GSAP
  • JavaScript

The design

The mission

The game needed a home page that feels like the game before anyone presses Play. The site tells the story, shows what's in the world, and links to the demo.

What I did

  • Forest scene: a 3D clearing with a smoking campfire, fireflies, ground mist, and a bunny and owl living there.
  • Field guide: weapons, animals and structures you can rotate and zoom, with day, dusk and night lighting. Night shows glowing eyes, torches and Moonsteel.
  • World map: a 3D map of six regions with drifting clouds. Picking a region flies the camera there.
  • Navigation: the menu highlights the section you're reading.

The architecture

3D
