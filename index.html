About me

I'm a fourth year student at Far Eastern University - Alabang, taking a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology with a specialization in web and mobile applications. I build full-stack web and mobile apps: the interface people use, the backend behind it, and the deployment that keeps it live.

I focus on web and mobile development, system design, UI/UX design and machine learning. Most of what I know comes from building real projects: the web platform for my capstone, gameplay systems for a Roblox team, Java plugins for a Minecraft server with thousands of players, and Lakbay Lokal, a trip planner I'm building on my own.

Personal project, in progress

Lakbay Lokal

A free trip planner for the Philippines that I'm building on my own. It turns where you're staying and how far you'll go into a timed day of small, local places, with travel time between each stop.

It only recommends real places from its own list of about 220 and picks checked places first, while Gemini only rewords stored facts. Plans can be edited, swapped for indoor places on rainy days, shown on a map, added to a calendar, and followed offline on the day.
